## The quality gate you must report after each phase

1. **Routes and features:** exactly what works and what remains development-only.
2. **Visual fidelity:** compared screenshots at 1440px and 390px; layout/headline/ribbon/image differences recorded.
3. **Search:** titles/descriptions/H1/canonical/real HTML, correct publishing/indexing, no duplicate/doorway content, genuine Devon reference where appropriate, intended question/answer usefulness.
4. **Technical/a11y:** lint/typecheck/build and relevant browser/form/keyboard tests, with honest result.
5. **Evidence/commercial readiness:** source-asset/rights gaps, attribution, unapproved claims, provider/consent state.
6. **Decision:** PASS / CONDITIONAL / BLOCKED with precise remaining tasks.
